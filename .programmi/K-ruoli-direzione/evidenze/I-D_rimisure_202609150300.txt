## (3) Presenze (sys_attendance)

### presenze: totale, registro, per identificativo, per nome, con/senza provenienza
```sql
select (select count(*) from sys.sys_attendance) as presenze,
       (select count(*) from sys.sys_source_lineage_records where replace(source_lineage_target_table_name,'sys.','')='sys_attendance') as nel_registro,
       (select count(*) from sys.sys_source_lineage_records where replace(source_lineage_target_table_name,'sys.','')='sys_attendance' and source_lineage_target_record_id is not null) as per_identificativo,
       (select count(*) from sys.sys_source_lineage_records where replace(source_lineage_target_table_name,'sys.','')='sys_attendance' and source_lineage_source_natural_key is not null) as per_nome,
       (select count(*) from sys.sys_attendance a where exists (select 1 from sys.sys_source_lineage_records l where replace(source_lineage_target_table_name,'sys.','')='sys_attendance' and l.source_lineage_target_record_id = a.attendance_id)) as presenze_con_provenienza,
       (select count(*) from sys.sys_attendance a where not exists (select 1 from sys.sys_source_lineage_records l where replace(source_lineage_target_table_name,'sys.','')='sys_attendance' and l.source_lineage_target_record_id = a.attendance_id)) as presenze_senza_provenienza
```

## (4) CONFLITTI PER D5 — quattro tabelle ibride
Definizione ESEGUITA (euristica, perche' la colonna di origine X-2 non esiste ancora): una riga e' «in conflitto» se
  (a) esiste nel registro di provenienza (e' stata importata) E (b) la tabella bersaglio ha updated_at > created_at del registro (e' stata modificata DOPO la corsa di importazione).
Il confronto e' bersaglio.updated_at > registro.created_at, NON un confronto interno al registro.

### sys_time_off_balances
```sql
select count(*) as righe_totali,
       count(*) filter (where l.source_lineage_record_id is not null) as importate_nel_registro,
       count(*) filter (where l.source_lineage_record_id is null) as native_senza_registro,
       count(*) filter (where l.source_lineage_record_id is not null and b.updated_at > l.created_at) as in_conflitto,
       count(*) filter (where l.source_lineage_record_id is not null and b.updated_at > l.created_at + interval '1 hour') as in_conflitto_oltre_1h,
       count(distinct b.balance_subject_user_id) filter (where l.source_lineage_record_id is not null and b.updated_at > l.created_at) as persone_o_tenant_coinvolti
```
